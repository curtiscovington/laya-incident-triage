# Third-party materials

The model under test is the independent Laya English checkpoint, published by
Convai Innovations at https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya under Apache-2.0.
Its pinned revision and SHA-256 are recorded in study/FREEZE.json. Model weights
are downloaded separately and are not included in this repository.

The `laya` runtime, PyTorch, NumPy, scikit-learn, SciPy, Matplotlib, Hugging Face
Hub tooling, and other dependencies retain their respective licenses. The
requirements and environment files identify versions; no dependency source or
installed virtual environment is redistributed here.

TypeSafe Jev inspired the interface. TypeSafe's model, weights, API, benchmarks,
and branding are not part of this experiment. This repository is not affiliated
with or endorsed by TypeSafe or the Laya authors.

All incident fixtures are synthetic and authored for this experiment. They do
not contain real production incident records or customer data.
